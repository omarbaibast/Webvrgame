A lightweight, self-contained 3D world starter designed to run on mobile browsers. Use your phone in a VR Box for split-screen viewing, or play in normal 2D mode.

DualShock 4 / Gamepad
Left stick: move   •   Right stick: look
Cross (✕): jump / fly   •   Circle (○): beacon

Keyboard: WASD move, arrows look, Space jump, B beacon, E wave, V toggle VR.

Important: This starter includes a local procedural world and local controls. Firebase multiplayer and remote players are not included in this replacement file.
